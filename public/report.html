Executive Summary

This study formulates, prices, and stress-tests a commercial parametric weather insurance product protecting 3.0 kVA residential rooftop solar installations in Ahmedabad (Pincode 380006). Using 20 years of ECMWF ERA5-Land reanalysis climate data (2005–2024), we construct a temperature-derated GHI index. A trigger threshold at 90% AEP50 (4,140 kWh) and exit floor at 70% AEP50 (3,220 kWh) yields a Pure Burn Cost of ₹75/yr, an Actuarially Loaded Premium of ₹116/yr (₹137 with 18% GST), and an embedded loan cost of ₹11/month (+1.8% on a typical ₹3,200 loan).
